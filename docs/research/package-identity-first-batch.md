Reviewed 2026-09-30. Scope: establish whether one existing ClearSip variant record can be tied to an **exact US package** using first-party evidence. This is a source audit, not an in-hand scan or a license to reuse manufacturer images.

## Decision and limits

This evidence is sufficient to mark **GTIN `00052000324815`, Gatorade Cool Blue, 20 fl oz / 591 mL bottle** as a *manufacturer-backed, package-specific web-label snapshot* with the label's September 12, 2026 update date and a September 30, 2026 access/review date. It is stronger than the current variant-wide row or an observed-size list because the GTIN, size, serving count, ingredients, and nutrition all point to the same manufacturer's product. It is **not** sufficient to say that a user-scanned physical bottle has been visually checked or that every package carrying this GTIN today has the same label. [PepsiCo warns](https://www.pepsicoproductfacts.com/Home/Product?gtin=00052000324815) that formulation and packaging may change and directs consumers to current store packaging. Accordingly, any app result should identify this as online manufacturer evidence and invite comparison with the bottle in hand, especially after a package or label update.

Do not propagate this package's one-serving or nutrient amounts to Gatorade's 12, 24, or 28 fl oz options. Do not use the product-page photos as training images or bundle them with the app on the basis of this audit. No physical barcode or packaging photo was captured in this research pass.
