# Core US beverage label audit

Reviewed 2026-09-26. Scope: the first 29 soda, sports drink, energy drink and water discoveries in `scripts/bootstrap_db.py`, from `coca-cola-original` through `lacroix`. The companion `data/reviewed-labels-core.json` contains 23 exact-variant ingredient statements reviewed against official US sources. It records source serving nutrition, not guessed whole-package amounts. All entries remain `scope: variant`; observed sizes do not establish exact package/UPC matches. Null means not established in this pass, not zero. No retailer or crowd-sourced ingredient list was imported.

## Powerade findings that affect the screenshot

[Powerade Zero's current official page](https://www.powerade.com/products/powerade-zero) contains a complete Mixed Berry ingredient panel. It declares sucralose and acesulfame potassium, plus electrolytes, vitamins B12/C, natural flavors, calcium disodium EDTA and Blue 1. It gives 0 calories, 0 g total sugars, 0 g added sugars and 240 mg sodium per **12 fl oz serving**. Sweetener amounts are not disclosed. The page lists 12/20/28 fl oz available sizes separately; that is not evidence that every bottle has the same serving count or verified package label.

[Regular Powerade's official page](https://www.powerade.com/products/powerade) gives Grape, Lemon Lime and Orange 80 calories, 21 g total and added sugars, and 240 mg sodium per 12 fl oz. The current Orange ingredients say **NATURAL FLAVORS**; the prior bootstrap transcription says natural and artificial flavors. The current source uses **YELLOW 5 AND 6**. The page's panels say 2.5 servings per 28 fl oz container; do not attach these to a 20 fl oz bottle simply because 20 fl oz appears in the availability list. No caffeine amount appears in these panels.

## Import boundaries

The four follow-up PepsiCo panels above were reached by clicking matching GTIN rows in the public service index. All show September 12, 2026 as their update date. Earlier `/services/labels/index` requests failed for these products; the index's own `/services/labels` links exposed the panels. Mountain Dew and Gatorade Water's serving volumes are supported by their matching GTIN Product Facts titles, not inferred from unrelated package-size lists. Caffeine values in these rows come from those matching GTIN Product Facts pages.

Ingredient statements preserve source order and collective parenthetical groups. Capitalization is retained; web whitespace is normalized. They are factual label transcriptions, not a copy of marketing descriptions. Caffeine and added sugars are null whenever the matching source does not expressly supply the value. The two official-source conflicts above are preserved rather than silently resolved. The PepsiCo Product Facts pages warn that formulations and packaging can change and that current store packaging controls. This pass does not convert those webpages into current in-hand package verification.
