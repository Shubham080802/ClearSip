# Diverse non-coffee beverage expansion batch

**Research date:** September 26, 2026  
**Scope:** 35 current, U.S.-facing, non-alcoholic product candidates from first-party manufacturer pages only. This is a diversification queue for ClearSip's **catalog-discovery** tier; it is not a claim that the catalog is nationally complete or that every candidate has a current package label attached.

## Import guardrails

- Import every row as `discovery_candidate` / `manufacturer_page`, never as a reviewed package label. Preserve the source URL, retrieval date, exact displayed name, size evidence, and the page's current status.
- `Not stated` means that the cited manufacturer page did not establish a net package size for that exact item. Do not fill it with a customary can or bottle size.
- A product-family claim (for example, a brand's “zero sugar” statement) is **not** an ingredient statement for each variant. Capture the exact package's Nutrition Facts, ingredient statement, UPC/GTIN, and label version before classification.
- Product availability, formulations, and flavors change. Seasonal/limited records should have an availability/recheck flag, not a permanent current-product assertion.

## Recommended ingestion order

1. Add all 35 as source-backed discoveries with the provenance above. That takes the catalog from 52 to **87 records** (2 reviewed package labels + 85 discoveries) without pretending those 35 are ingredient-verified.
2. Promote the 14 rows with explicit ingredient/Nutrition Facts pages first: Ocean Spray #1–8, V8 #10–14, and Oatly #29. A promotion still needs an exact current package/UPC or other reviewed package match.
3. Prioritize an individual current label/GTIN for Waterloo, Hint, V8 Zero Sugar #15–16, Oatly #30–32, and OLIPOP. Their listed family/page evidence supports discovery, not a nutrition or ingredient assessment.
4. In the classifier, model **product format** as well as flavor: shelf-stable/refrigerated, concentration/preparation basis where applicable, and the exact Oatly/OLIPOP formula tier. A name-only match is insufficient.

## Classification cautions

- Do not translate a beverage's ingredient presence, “zero sugar,” “no added sugar,” “no artificial sweetener,” nutrient, or caffeine declaration into a broad safe/unsafe, disease, insulin, or medical-suitability conclusion.
- For the V8 energy rows, record stated 80 mg caffeine per listed can where the page gives it; that is a label fact, not an individual intake recommendation.
- The current pages include marketing language. Retain only auditable label facts and source links in the dataset; do not copy marketing copy or imply that a brand claim has been independently validated.
