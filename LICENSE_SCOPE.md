## Beverage data is separate

The repository does **not** offer a blanket MIT or CC0 license for its beverage
dataset. This applies to manufacturer-derived facts, label transcriptions,
source excerpts, and imported product records wherever they occur, including
`data/reviewed-labels-*.json`, seed data embedded in `scripts/bootstrap_db.py`
and `src/data.js`, and third-party material quoted in research notes.
Original schema definitions, importers, and assessment code remain MIT-licensed.

Source URLs and access dates document provenance, **not permission to
redistribute**. An ingredient-quality review is not a rights-clearance review.
No blanket manufacturer redistribution permissions are recorded by this notice.
Before exporting, commercializing, or expanding source-derived collections,
review each source's terms and applicable rights, and obtain permission where
required. A code license does not resolve those questions.

USDA FoodData Central identifies its own data as public domain, published under
CC0 1.0, and requests source attribution. Those terms apply to records actually
obtained from FDC—not automatically to ClearSip's manufacturer-page snapshots.
Keep the FDC ID, release/access date, and source attribution with imported data.
See the [USDA licensing statement](https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/api-guide/#bkmk-4).

For current source provenance and limitations, see the
[catalog audit](docs/research/catalog-availability-audit.md) and
[source acquisition plan](docs/research/us-beverage-catalog-sources.md).
