THE CLEARSIP STANDARD

A label deserves more than a red or green score.

01

Read the package

We identify the product, market, size, and label version—because those details can change the answer.

02

Explain, don’t alarm

Ingredients are categorized by function and evidence. Unknown quantities stay clearly marked as unknown.

03

Show the work

Sources and review status travel with each product, so you can decide what you trust.
